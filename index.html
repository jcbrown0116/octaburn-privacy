Privacy Policy for OCTABURN/HIITBURN

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

OCTABURN/HIITBURN ("the App") respects your privacy and collects zero personal data.

1. Zero Data Collection

OCTABURN does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, analytics, device identifiers, location data, or advertising IDs. No account or login is required, and the App contains no ads or tracking SDKs.

2. 100% On-Device Audio Processing

All music selection, waveform analysis, drop detection, and MP3 workout mix exports happen entirely locally on your device. Audio files are never uploaded to any server.

3. Local Preferences

Theme preferences (Dark/Light mode) are saved strictly locally on your device.
